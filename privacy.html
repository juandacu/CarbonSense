Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 December 2025

Carbon Sense (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website carbonsense.net. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information directly on carbonsense.net through forms, cookies, or on site tracking tools. However, you may choose to provide personal information in two ways.

1.1 Newsletter Subscriptions (via Substack)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you are redirected to our newsletter provider, Substack. Any information you provide there, such as your email address, is collected and processed in accordance with Substack’s own privacy policy.

We have access only to the email addresses of subscribed users for the purpose of sending newsletters and managing the mailing list.

1.2 Emails Sent to Us

If you contact us at info@carbonsense.net, we will receive the personal information you include in your message, such as your name, email address, and any other details you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only for the following purposes.

  • Sending newsletters to subscribers who have signed up via Substack
  • Responding to emails and enquiries you send to us

We do not do any of the following.

  • Sell personal information
  • Share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Use tracking cookies or analytics tools on carbonsense.net

3. Data Retention

We retain subscriber information and email correspondence only for as long as necessary to operate our newsletter or respond to your enquiries, or as required by applicable law.

You may request that we delete your information at any time, as set out below.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to do the following.

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw your consent to receive our newsletter

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask questions about how we handle your data, please email us at info@carbonsense.net.

5. Third Party Services

Newsletter subscriptions are managed by Substack, which may collect additional information such as your IP address and device information when you interact with their platform.

We do not control how Substack processes your data and recommend that you review Substack’s own privacy policy for full details of their practices.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal obligations. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us at:

info@carbonsense.net
